# Shah Evidence Score (SES) v1.0.0 — implementation specification

Status: **PROPOSED / DRAFT INTEGRATION**, October 18, 2026. This is a
versioned independent module and test suite, NOT yet a deployed change to
the authenticated seven-analyst Shah TradingAgents MCP backend.

## Core contract

- Seven analysts: market, social, news, fundamentals, flow, OI, dealer.
- Each supplies a **human- or provider-verified** direction in [-1,+1].
  Null means unavailable. A verified neutral score is 0. Never impute null as 0.
- The evaluator does **not** infer score direction from freeform LLM prose.
  Caller owns structured, cited scoring rubric and must record raw evidence.
- Base weights (sum 1.00): market .22, social .06, news .10,
  fundamentals .24, flow .16, OI .10, dealer .12. These are hypothesis weights,
  not optimized weights and not the historical EGHT dynamic weights.
- EffectiveWeight = BaseWeight x confidence x quality x freshness x
  overlapMultiplier, each multiplier in [0,1].
- Shared source event IDs for derivative analysts receive a second explicit
  50% incremental discount. Overlap is detected on raw trade/event IDs, not
  merely because data was acquired from the same vendor.
- Score = SUM(direction x effectiveWeight) / SUM(effectiveWeight). An empty
  denominator returns null, never an apparent neutral.
- HistoricalEffectiveWeight overrides exist ONLY to reproduce already-issued
  historical scorecards; they must never be used for prospective calibration.
- Report component scores, factors, timestamp/source IDs, effective weights,
  contributions, covered/missing analysts, diagnostic flags, SES version,
  overall category, and independent trade readiness.

## Scoring rubrics (requirements for upstream analyst adapters)

Market: Multi-timeframe trend, momentum and major support/resistance, with
verified close, technical sample and calculation timestamps. Use price
structure rather than a standalone RSI reading.

Social: Representative sample counts, source/time range and bot/duplicate
filter. Missing/one-post social sentiment yields null or low confidence.

News: Only verified company events, material macro/catalysts, release versus
publication date and explicit decay; opinion without evidence gets low quality.

Fundamentals: Period-matched filing figures, revenue/profit/cash quality,
balance-sheet strength, normalized recurring versus one-off items, valuation
and forward catalyst. Incomplete valuation gets a quality discount.

Flow: Only qualifying single-leg ask/bid SWEEP/BLOCK executions may carry
directional bias. Aggregate volume, OI, MULTI, and bid/ask labels by themselves
are not proof of openings or bullish/bearish intent. Record sample coverage,
trade timestamps, execution side, option contract and premium.

OI: Raw OI magnitude, 1/5/10/15/20 session persistence, structure,
spreads/rolls, and MULTI participation MAY support context and confidence.
Nonzero directional scoring REQUIRES confirmed OI changes plus qualifying
single-leg flow. No directional inference from call OI greater than put OI.
Full-strike 0-60 DTE is the minimum connected service's current scope.

Dealer: GEX/VEX are modeled exposures, not verified actual inventory. State
spot reference, calculation time, exposure units, strike and expiry, modeling
basis, missing volume-weighted GEX, and contradictions between feeds.
Gamma sign alone is not a bullish/bearish dealer prediction.

## Readiness and thresholds

Intervals:
- [-1.00, -0.50] Strong bearish
- (-0.50, -0.20] Bearish
- (-0.20, +0.20) Neutral
- [+0.20, +0.50) Bullish
- [+0.50, +1.00] Strong bullish

Reported data states:
- INSUFFICIENT if no weighted evidence, required analyst missing,
  or fewer than 60% of prior-weight slots assessed.
- PROVISIONAL if some analyst input is missing or effective evidence support
  is below 50% of base-weight budget.
- READY otherwise. Timestamp omissions always generate audit flags; until
  timestamp policy is verified by the upstream service, the trade gate
  independently requires a fresh quote and derivatives input.

Trade-decision gate is distinct from SES. Fail closed to NO_TRADE unless
relevant quote, technical confirmation, liquidity/spread, reward/risk,
earnings/event gap risk and (for options) fresh derivative data are all
**explicitly** true. Options trades require reported flow and OI components.
READY + all gates + abs(SES)>=.50 => CANDIDATE_FOR_REVIEW, NOT auto-order;
lower |SES| => WATCH. Portfolio-management Buy/Underweight/Sell remains a
separate human/PM judgment. This module does not execute trades.

## Regression baseline

The previously completed EGHT seven-agent run reported SES-like -0.331. Its
recorded analyst scores and effective weights mathematically reproduce
-0.3306095 (rounded -0.331). This does not assert that legacy weights were
generated by the new SES v1.0 formula.

Earlier AAPL +0.31 was an **illustrative manual score**, not an official
Shah seven-analyst result. Arithmetic is regression tested without treating
it as evidence that AAPL should receive that score from SES v1.0.

## Calibration/backtesting before production

1. Persist point-in-time scored evidence and market snapshot before outcomes
   are known. Lock scorer and thresholds by semantic version.
2. Apply next-executable-bar entry, market-specific fees/spread, no future
   information, realistic corporate actions and delistings, and feasible
   short/borrow/options assumptions.
3. Use walk-forward train/validate/test splits with purging and embargo for
   overlapping horizons. Cluster/bootstrap by date and underlying to avoid
   misleading independent samples.
4. Evaluate direction, calibration, hit rates, opportunity cost, drawdowns,
   transaction costs, stability by analyst, market regime, and evidence-quality
   bucket. Do not optimize thresholds on the holdout test set.
5. The included point-in-time harness checks input dates, labels and returns,
   and marks buckets with fewer than 30 observations NOT READY. Thirty is a
   diagnostic minimum, not proof of statistical significance.
6. Zero real outcome datasets were provided in this implementation.
   Therefore no historical performance, alpha or predictive reliability
   is claimed.
